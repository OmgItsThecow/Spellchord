Electric Guitar Multisamples Attribution
========================================

The WAV samples in this directory (E2, Ab2, C3, E3, Ab3, C4, E4, Ab4, C5, E5)
are derived from the FluidR3_GM SoundFont (`electric_guitar_jazz`) as
distributed in gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts under the MIT License.

Original SoundFont: FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen (MIT License).
Converted to 22,050 Hz 16-bit mono PCM WAV for in-app playback.
